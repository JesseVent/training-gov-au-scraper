Occupation Shortage

Jobs and Skills Australia leads work on occupation shortage analysis including the annual Occupation Shortage List and reports on occupation shortages.

    Occupation Shortage Drivers

    The OSD data is available as Excel spreadsheets.

    The Occupation Shortage Drivers classify shortage into three main drivers based on the likeliest cause of shortage. The three categories are: 

    • Education and training gap
    • Retention gap
    • Suitability gap.
